## TL;DR

This site uses minimal analytics and performance monitoring, but no ads or invasive tracking.

## Overview

Thanks for stopping by. This is my personal portfolio where I share my work, projects, and experience. I care about privacy, so this page explains what data may be collected and why.

## What this site collects

Most of this site is static. There are no user accounts, no ads, and no third-party trackers used for advertising or remarketing.

There are only a few situations where data may be processed:

### Analytics and performance

This site uses Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights to understand aggregate traffic patterns and site performance. This helps me see which pages are being used and whether the site is loading reliably.

### Contacting me

If you email me or use the contact form, I receive the name, email address, and message you submit so I can reply. Contact form submissions are processed through Resend for email delivery.

## How I use the info

Any information that’s processed is used to:

- Keep the site running properly
- Monitor overall traffic and site performance
- Improve the site and content
- Respond to messages and feedback

That’s it.

I don’t sell, trade, or rent personal data. If you accidentally share something you’d rather not have stored, email me and I’ll do my best to remove it.
